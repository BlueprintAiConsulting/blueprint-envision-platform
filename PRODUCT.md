# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Homeowners in Pennsylvania (West Chester, Pottstown, Chester & Montgomery Counties) seeking roof or siding replacements, and professional roofing sales specialists / project managers conducting in-home consultations and field takeoffs.

## Product Purpose

Instant AI-powered residential exterior visualization platform. Enables homeowners and contractors to preview authentic CertainTeed Landmark® shingles, Monogram® vinyl siding, and designer trims on real property photos or Google Street View in 30 seconds, calculate 4-side field takeoffs, and generate quote specifications.

## Brand Personality

Architectural, authoritative, reliable, tactile, and clear. Grounded in contractor craftsmanship and real-world manufacturer product lines.

## Anti-references

- Faux 3D low-poly cartoon renderers
- Unreadable washed-out gray text on dark surfaces
- Flashing neon gradient text and gimmicky decorative clutter
- Clunky multi-step signup walls before seeing any visualization
- Ambiguous swatch palettes with non-standardized colors

## Design Principles

1. **Photorealistic Truth**: Visualizations must honor actual architectural lighting, shingle dimensionality, and authentic manufacturer color swatches.
2. **Frictionless Velocity**: Homeowners should see their home transformed in under 30 seconds from address entry or mobile camera snap with zero required login.
3. **Field-Ready Precision**: UI elements must be easily operable on mobile devices and iPads under bright sunlight or kitchen-table lighting conditions.
4. **Transparent Specification**: Quotes and takeoffs must map directly to real product SKUs, squares, and warranties without hidden math.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA compliance across all studio controls, contrast ratios ≥ 4.5:1 for body and ≥ 3:1 for large display text.
- Minimum 44x44px touch targets on mobile viewports.
- Keyboard accessible navigation and accessible labels for screen readers.
- Motion-safe alternatives for users with prefers-reduced-motion.
